# tower-defense
https://nnkd-kevin.itch.io/an-ordinary-tower-defense-game
## I. What is this project ?
- This is a strategic tower defense game I've made in Godot game engine with my friend [philogamer](https://github.com/philogamer) for a week. Where you place down guarding towers to defend your kingdom from the incoming invasion of monsters in a fantasy setting.

## II.Features:
- The game takes on a traditional tower defense core loop with 6 unique towers you can use to defend your kingdom:
- Crossbow tower: The standard tower that shoots arrow, with the number of projectile increases per level.
- Lightning tower: The arcane tower that shoots lightning orbs from its magic core, dealing high damage and stun enemies.
- Bomb tower: The arcane tower that charges to generate a magical orb out of mana in the air and launch them towards enemies, causing massive aoe destruction.
- Light tower: The tower that embodies the holy energy, charging to shoot fragment of light that sends the enemies back in a short period of time.
- Golden tower: The ultimate DPS tower that is costly to upgrade, but gives you a reliable defend with its absolute firepower.
- Fire tower: The tower that starts off average but builds up to crazy destruction to tanky enemies and bosses as its projectiles stack burn damage on enemies, causing them to take heavy damage as the burn stack increases.
